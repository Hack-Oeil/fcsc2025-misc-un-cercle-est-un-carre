Challenge #572

A circle is a square

Alice and Bob live on a peculiar planet: it is neither flat nor spherical, it's a cube! They want to optimize their wired surface connection in order to minimize communication latency.

The cube has dimensions 32x32x32 and the format of the points where Alice and Bob live is as follows: [0,2,14] or [32,28,11]. Note that these points are indeed located on the surface of their planet.

To minimize wired communications, Alice and Bob need to find the shortest distance on the surface between a series of pairs of points. To simplify calculations and keep only integer values, the square of the shortest path distance (on the surface) between each pair of points is expected.

Taking the previous example, the expected minimum distance path between points [0,2,14] and [32,28,11] would be 3789. This path passes through the point [13,0,0].

Note: the provided file un-cercle-est-un-carre.py is the server-side code that answers the service. We do not provide the minimum_distance.py file. It is referenced because it is used to verify your answers: your goal is to write the minimumDistanceOnCube function.
